# Kapda Portal — Admin Guide

Updated 20 Nov 2026

Kapda Portal keeps track of every piece of clothing in the shop: what arrives from suppliers, what is on the shelf in each size, and what sells, at what price and profit. Stock goes up and down by itself with every delivery and every sale, so the numbers always match the shelf.

## Getting started

**Opening it.** Open the portal's link in any browser on a phone, tablet or computer. On a phone, use the browser's **Add to Home Screen** so it opens like an app.

**Moving around.** On a phone or tablet, use the tab bar at the bottom: **Home, Sell, Stock, Deliveries, More** (More holds every other page). On a computer, use the menu on the left. The **Help** page (the ? icon at the top on a phone) explains everything again in plain words.

**Setting up your real shop (once).**

1. **Settings**: type your name, shop name, address and GSTIN (printed on bills). Press **Start fresh with my own data** to remove the sample shop.
2. **Suppliers**: add the mills and wholesalers you buy from.
3. **My stock → Add a product**: add each item with its prices and how many pieces of each size you have today.
4. Start selling. From now on the portal keeps the counts for you.

The current version saves everything in the browser on that one device. Use **Settings → Download backup** regularly until it is connected to a database.

## The daily workflow

Every day is the same loop: stock comes in, it sits on the shelf, it sells, and what runs low gets ordered again.

**Order stock** (Deliveries) → **Stock arrives** (Deliveries) → **On the shelf** (My stock) → **Sold** (Make a sale) → **Runs low** (Home, Reports) → reorder what runs low, and the loop starts again.

Each step happens on the page named in brackets; the counts update by themselves at every arrow.

- **Morning**: open **Home**. Read the **Needs your attention** list: sold-out sizes, sizes running low, late deliveries, deliveries due soon and slow sellers. Tap any line to go straight to it.
- **When a customer buys**: **Sell** → tap each size → check the bill → choose how they paid → **Complete sale**. Print or move on to the next sale.
- **When a delivery comes in**: **Deliveries** → **Stock arrived** (or open the order you placed and press **Goods arrived**). Count the boxes and enter what actually came.
- **When something is returned, damaged or miscounted**: open the bill to take a return, or open the product and use **Adjust stock**.
- **Evening**: check today's total on **Home**, and glance at **Reports → What to reorder**.
- **Once a week**: order what Reports suggests (**Deliveries → Order stock**), follow up late deliveries, collect pay-later amounts (shown under **Customers**), and **Download backup** in Settings.

## How to do the common jobs

**Make a sale.** Sell → tap a size once per piece → check the bill (on a phone, tap **Review & pay** in the dark bar) → choose Paid by → optionally pick the customer → **Complete sale** → Print bill or Next sale.

**Record stock that has arrived.** Deliveries → **Stock arrived** → pick the supplier → choose each product → type how many of each size came → **Save & add to stock**.

**Order stock and receive it later.** Deliveries → **Order stock** → supplier, expected date, products and quantities → **Save order**. When the supplier sends it, open the order → **It's been dispatched**. When it arrives → **Goods arrived** → enter what actually came (anything short stays on order).

**Add a new product.** My stock → **Add a product** → name, category, colour → You buy at, MRP, You sell at → pick the size type and type today's count per size → **Add product**. The style code is filled in for you; brand, fabric, supplier, rack and alert level are under **More details**.

**Take a return.** Past sales → search the bill → open it → **Return** next to the item → how many and why → **Return to stock**. The refund amount is shown.

**Fix a wrong count, or remove damaged pieces.** My stock → tap the product → **Adjust stock** → size → what happened → how many → note → **Save adjustment**.

**Find or print an old bill.** Past sales → search by bill number, name or phone → open → **Print invoice**.

**Decide what to reorder.** Reports → **What to reorder** shows each size's selling speed, when it runs out and how many to order. Download it as CSV to send to the supplier.

**Stop selling a product.** Open it → **Archive**. It disappears from Sell and My stock but its history stays; restore it from More filters → Show archived products.

## Good to know

**The portal protects your numbers.** It will not let you sell more pieces than you have, receive more than was ordered, return more than was bought, or take stock below zero. Quantities are always whole pieces. A selling price can't be above MRP. A product that has been sold can only be archived, not deleted. Deleting, cancelling and starting fresh always ask first.

**Every change is recorded.** Sales, deliveries, returns and corrections each write a line in **Stock history**, so any count can be explained. **Settings → Check my data** confirms that every number adds up.

**Keep your data safe.** Use **Download backup** in Settings at least weekly, and keep the file somewhere safe. **Restore backup** brings everything back from that file.

**Words used in the portal**

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Stock | Pieces in the shop right now |
| SKU / style code | A product's own code; each size gets its own code (e.g. KP-TSH-1001-M) |
| MRP | Highest price printed on the tag |
| You buy at / You sell at | What you pay the supplier for one piece / what the customer pays you |
| Profit / margin | Selling price minus buying price (rent and salaries not included) |
| GST | Tax added to the bill, shown as CGST + SGST; clothes up to ₹2,500 a piece are usually 5% |
| HSN code | The GST category number printed on bills |
| Delivery / supplier order | Stock bought from a supplier, arrived or not yet |
| Pay later | A sale on credit; the amount to collect shows on the customer |
| Sell-through | Out of every 100 pieces bought, how many have sold |
| Archive | Hide a product you no longer sell, keeping its history |
